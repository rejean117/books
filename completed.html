Possessive Series 11: Valerian Volkzki

Valerian Volkzki, a powerful and intimidating figure, finds his world turned upside down when he becomes infatuated with Grace Oquendo, a veterinarian. Despite his reputation and the warnings from those around him, Valerian is drawn to Grace's charm and resilience. As he grapples with his feelings, he must confront the walls he's built around himself and decide if he's willing to let someone in.

Possessive Series 12: Cali Sudalga

Cali Sudalga has it all—wealth, luxury cars, yachts, and a mansion that many dream of. Despite his material success, he's emotionally closed off, scarred by a past betrayal from the only woman who ever broke through his defenses. Two years later, she reappears in his life, pretending as if nothing happened. Cali must navigate his lingering feelings and decide whether to confront the past or continue guarding his heart.

Temptation Island 5: Desidero Me, Amore Mio

Iris Gonzaga-Racini is trapped in a loveless marriage, enduring a life filled with suffering. Her world shifts when she receives a mysterious black invitation with gold lettering: "You are invited to Temptation Island." Owned by the enigmatic Monasterio brothers, the island offers a space where individuals can explore their deepest desires. As Iris steps into this world of liberation and self-discovery, she confronts her own suppressed fantasies and the possibility of a new beginning.
