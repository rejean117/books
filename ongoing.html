On Going

Possessive Series 11: Valerian Volkzki

Synopsis:Valerian Volkzki is a powerful and disciplined man who thrives on control and order. However, his structured life begins to unravel when he becomes inexplicably drawn to Grace Oquendo, a woman who challenges his self-imposed boundaries. Despite his efforts to maintain distance, Valerian finds himself obsessing over Grace's presence, scent, and allure. Her mixed Japanese and Spanish heritage adds to her mystique, further complicating Valerian's emotions. As he grapples with his desires, Valerian must confront the possibility that his carefully curated world is being upended by love and lust.

Possessive Series 12: Cali Sudalga

Synopsis:Cali Sudalga has it all—wealth, luxury cars, yachts, and a mansion that epitomizes opulence. Despite his material success, he remains emotionally guarded, scarred by a past betrayal from the only woman who ever broke through his defenses. Two years after she left him for another man, she reappears in his life, feigning ignorance of their shared history. Cali is forced to confront unresolved feelings and the pain of deception as he navigates the complexities of trust and vulnerability. .

Knock Knock Professor

Synopsis: The story follows Xildius Vouganville, known as XV, a brilliant professor who solves mysterious cases using his expertise in anatomy and psychology. Due to a unique condition, he remains confined within his mansion, Villa Vouganville. Fift Salustiana, enticed by a lucrative salary advertised on a flyer, becomes his new assistant. As she delves into her role, Fift uncovers the peculiar nature of XV's profession and finds herself drawn to the intense allure he exudes. The narrative explores whether Fift can bring light into the professor's shadowed existence.
