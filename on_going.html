On Going

Knock Knock Professor

Synopsis: The story follows Xildius Vouganville, known as XV, a brilliant professor who solves mysterious cases using his expertise in anatomy and psychology. Due to a unique condition, he remains confined within his mansion, Villa Vouganville. Fift Salustiana, enticed by a lucrative salary advertised on a flyer, becomes his new assistant. As she delves into her role, Fift uncovers the peculiar nature of XV's profession and finds herself drawn to the intense allure he exudes. The narrative explores whether Fift can bring light into the professor's shadowed existence.
